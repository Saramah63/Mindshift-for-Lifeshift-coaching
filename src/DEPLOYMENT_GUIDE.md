### Step 2: Deploy Your Website to www.saramahmodi.com

Choose one of these hosting platforms:

#### Option A: Vercel (Recommended - Free & Easy)

1. **Sign up:**
   - Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com)
   - Sign up with GitHub, GitLab, or email

2. **Deploy your project:**
   - Upload your code to GitHub (or use Vercel CLI)
   - Import your GitHub repository to Vercel
   - Vercel will automatically build and deploy

3. **Connect your domain:**
   - In Vercel dashboard, go to your project
   - Click "Settings" → "Domains"
   - Add `saramahmodi.com` and `www.saramahmodi.com`
   - Vercel will provide DNS records

4. **Update DNS settings:**
   - Go to your domain registrar (where you bought saramahmodi.com)
   - Add the DNS records Vercel provides:
     ```
     Type: A
     Name: @
     Value: 76.76.21.21
     
     Type: CNAME
     Name: www
     Value: cname.vercel-dns.com
     ```
   - Wait 24-48 hours for DNS propagation

**Pros:** Free, automatic SSL, fast global CDN, automatic deployments

#### Option B: Netlify (Also Great & Free)

1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com)
2. Sign up and connect your GitHub repository
3. Or drag-drop your build folder
4. Add custom domain in "Domain settings"
5. Update DNS at your domain registrar

**Pros:** Free tier, easy drag-and-drop, form handling included

#### Option C: Your Domain Hosting (If included with domain)

1. Check if your domain provider offers hosting
2. Build your project: `npm run build`
3. Upload the `dist` folder via FTP or File Manager
4. Your site will be live at www.saramahmodi.com

### Step 3: Submit to Google Search Console

1. **Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console)**

2. **Add your property:**
   - Click "Add Property"
   - Choose "Domain" property
   - Enter: `saramahmodi.com` (without www)

3. **Verify ownership via DNS:**
   - Google will give you a TXT record
   - Go to your domain registrar's DNS settings
   - Add the TXT record Google provides
   - Click "Verify" in Google Search Console

4. **Submit your sitemap:**
   - In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps"
   - Enter: `https://www.saramahmodi.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

5. **Request indexing:**
   - Go to "URL Inspection"
   - Enter: `https://www.saramahmodi.com`
   - Click "Request Indexing"
   - Repeat for key pages
