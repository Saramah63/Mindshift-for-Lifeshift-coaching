## 🚨 Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Buying backlinks
- ❌ Keyword stuffing
- ❌ Duplicate content
- ❌ Ignoring mobile optimization
- ❌ Slow page load times
- ❌ Missing meta descriptions
- ❌ Broken links
- ❌ No alt text on images
- ❌ Thin content (pages with <300 words)
- ❌ Not updating content regularly
