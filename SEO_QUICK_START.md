### ☐ 2. Google Search Console Setup (20 minutes)
**Most Important SEO Action!**

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with Gmail account (use saramah63@gmail.com)
3. Click "Add Property"
4. Enter: `https://www.saramahmodi.com`
5. Choose verification method:
   - **Option A:** HTML tag (easiest)
   - **Option B:** DNS record
6. Complete verification
7. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" (left menu)
8. Submit sitemap: `sitemap.xml`
9. Click "Submit"

**Expected Result:** Google will start indexing your site within 3-7 days

## 🚫 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Don't:
1. Ignore Google Search Console errors
2. Forget to update content
3. Buy backlinks or fake reviews
4. Stuff keywords unnaturally
5. Ignore social media completely
6. Set and forget (SEO needs maintenance)
7. Expect instant results (SEO takes time!)
8. Copy content from other sites
9. Neglect mobile experience
10. Ignore analytics data

### ✅ Do:
1. Check Search Console weekly
2. Update content quarterly
3. Earn backlinks naturally
4. Write naturally for humans
5. Stay active on social media
6. Review and adjust monthly
7. Be patient (3-6 months for results)
8. Create original content
9. Test on mobile regularly
10. Learn from your data
